# Card data notice

`dominion.json` is a snapshot of the card and edition data from
https://github.com/dennisgermany/dominion (file `dominion.json`, commit
`c1a34a709b9759c4347fa42f2a4bdc47b74f6fd2`, 2026-09-02), used under the MIT License below.
It contains card names (English and German), costs, types and edition membership only: no card text or artwork.

Dominion is a trademark of its respective owners. This project is a fan-made helper and is not affiliated
with or endorsed by them.

`corrections.json` records fixes to the snapshot that were confirmed by comparing it with other public
card lists. The snapshot itself is kept exactly as published; the importer applies the corrections on top.
